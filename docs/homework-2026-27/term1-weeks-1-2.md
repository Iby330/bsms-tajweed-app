# Homework 2026/27 — Term 1, weeks 1–2 (draft for review)

Six homeworks cover the first two weeks for every class except Basics:

| Week | Old structure (Beginner / Intermediate) | New structure (Advanced / Returning) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Ghunna 1 · Umm al-Kitāb 1 | Ghunna 1 · Mudūd 1 |
| 2 | Ghunna 2 · Umm al-Kitāb 2 | Ghunna 2 · Mudūd 2 |

**Approach.** Ghunna and Mudūd keep last year's questions. A question changes only
where an interactive format does the job better, or where the video teaches
something the paper never asks. Umm al-Kitāb is new, written from the video
transcripts.

**Legend.** *Kept* = last year's question, unchanged. *Changed* = same knowledge,
new format. *New* = fills a gap in the video. *Fixed* = something was broken.

**Quranic text.** Every passage and Quranic option is pulled from the app's own
word-by-word mushaf data when it's seeded, never typed out by hand. Tap-the-rule
answer keys come from the sourced tajweed annotation set, not from whoever wrote
the question.
